# Monday Coffee Expansion Project

## Objective
This project explores the sales data of Monday Coffee, a brand that has been selling its products online since January 2023. The aim is to identify the top three major Indian cities where opening new coffee shop locations would be most strategic—based on consumer demand, sales performance, and overall market potential.


## Key Questions
11. **Coffee Consumer Estimate**
How many people in each city are likely coffee consumers, assuming 25% of the population drinks coffee?

2. **Total Revenue (Q4 2023)**
What is the total revenue generated from coffee sales across all cities in the last quarter of 2023?

3. **Product Sales Volume**
How many units of each coffee product have been sold?

4. **City-wise Average Sales per Customer**
What is the average purchase value per customer in each city?

5. **City Demographics & Coffee Drinkers**
What are the populations and estimated coffee-drinking populations of each city?

6. **Top-Selling Products by City**
What are the top 3 best-selling products in each city?

7. **Unique Customers by City**
How many unique customers have purchased coffee in each city?

8. **Sales vs. Rent Analysis**
What is the average sale per customer vs. average rent per customer in each city?

9. **Monthly Sales Growth**
How is sales performance evolving month-over-month? What is the growth or decline trend?

10. **Market Potential**
Which three cities have the strongest market potential based on total sales, rental cost, customer count, and coffee consumption estimates?

Based on the analysis, the top three recommended cities for opening new Monday Coffee outlets are:

**Pune**
1. Highest total revenue among all cities
2. High average sales per customer
3. Low average rent per customer

**Delhi**
1. Estimated 7.7 million coffee consumers—the highest among all cities
2. Highest number of unique customers (68)
3. Rent per customer remains reasonable at ₹330

**Jaipur**
1. Highest number of unique customers (69)
2. Very low rent per customer (₹156)
3. Strong average sales per customer (₹11.6K)
